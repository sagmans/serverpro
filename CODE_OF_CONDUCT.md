# Code of Conduct

serverpro uses the Contributor Covenant standard in spirit: be respectful,
constructive, and safe to collaborate with.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming language.
- Assume good intent, but fix harm quickly.
- Keep feedback technical and actionable.
- Respect differing experience levels and backgrounds.
- Avoid publishing private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, hate speech, or sexualized attention.
- Insults, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Sharing private contact, account, host, IP, credential, or vulnerability
  details without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues or PRs; block
participants; or report abuse to platform operators. Security-sensitive reports
must use `SECURITY.md`.
